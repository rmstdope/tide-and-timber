# Tide and Timber: Working Story Outline

Status: proposed progression for discussion, not an implementation plan.

## Relationship to the master document

[Game Vision](game-vision.md) is the authority for agreed story and mechanics.
This outline develops the proposed sequence from the beach through four engine
rooms to departure. New details here, including growing methods, dungeon themes,
reveal timing, and individual scenes, need agreement before becoming requirements.

These are progression stages, not necessarily chapters shown to the player.
There are no fixed day counts. Quiet days, relationships, and home improvements
should fit between major discoveries without an implied countdown.

The [island map](island-map.md) places every stage, the four buried engines,
farming gates, and return routes in a proposed geography.

## The shape of the journey

| Part | Stages | The player's changing goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finding a home | 1-3 | Survive, find people, and earn a place to settle. |
| Learning to heal | 4-5 | Understand the darkness and reclaim the first route. |
| Following the hum | 6-9 | Investigate what lies beneath the island and awaken the first engine. |
| Discovering the ark | 10-13 | Restore the other engines and understand what the island really is. |
| Answering the call | 14-16 | Prepare the final remedy and free the ark from the darkness. |
| Carrying home forward | 17-18 | Leave together and fulfil the wandering garden's purpose. |

## Part I: Finding a home

### 1. The first night

- **Story:** The protagonist washes ashore without memories. Approaching nightfall
  makes the beach the sensible place to stay. A faint vibration through the sand
  is an unexplained detail, not an announcement of destiny.
- **Gameplay:** Salvage simple materials, gather food, and assemble shelter for
  the night. Teach only what is needed now; do not require a farm or a permanent
  beach settlement.
- **Progression:** A sheltered first night gives way to the search for fresh
  water and other people. The opening establishes vulnerability without a
  punishing timed survival test.

### 2. A path through the woods

- **Story:** Signs of maintained land interrupt the apparent wilderness: a cut
  branch, a repaired crossing, a tended plant. A villager encountered along the
  route leads or directs the protagonist to the village.
- **Gameplay:** Explore a short woodland route, gather useful materials, and
  learn to recognise landmarks. Save the introduction of dark creatures for a
  moment when the player understands what is at stake.
- **Progression:** Reach an established community, not an empty settlement the
  player must build from nothing.

### 3. A place to belong

- **Story:** The village offers a neglected dwelling and nearby growing space.
  Its inhabitants have work and concerns of their own. Help and hospitality
  begin relationships; nobody immediately declares the newcomer a saviour.
- **Gameplay:** Repair the essentials, plant familiar food crops, gather, and
  share a first harvest. Introduce village knowledge and the existence of a
  small library without making research compulsory yet.
- **Progression:** Establish a dependable base for expeditions. The beach
  shelter remains part of the arrival story, not a second mandatory homestead.

## Part II: Learning to heal

### 4. The forest that will not grow

- **Story:** Deeper in the woods, healthy growth gives way to a dark obstruction
  and hostile creatures. Villagers recognise the danger but have only partial
  knowledge of ways to treat it.
- **Gameplay:** Introduce manageable combat and the option to retreat. Show
  clearly that defeating a creature does not cure the affected ground. A clue
  and a surviving plant on the reachable side suggest a remedy.
- **Progression:** Give the player a concrete reason to develop the farm:
  reclaim a route that weapons alone cannot open.

### 5. The first remedy

- **Story:** Working with village knowledge, the protagonist cultivates a plant
  whose preparation can treat this local darkness. The achievement comes from
  care and learning, not unexplained powers.
- **Gameplay:** Grow and prepare the remedy, return to the obstruction, fight
  through to its local source, and apply the treatment. Keep this first
  farming-to-expedition loop short and understandable.
- **Progression:** The treated route stays clear and opens access to the jungle.
  Living plants and returning wildlife make the change tangible. Removing a
  later harvest does not undo the cure.

## Part III: Following the hum

### 6. Something beneath the roots

- **Story:** In the jungle, the protagonist hears humming through the soil.
  Exposed surfaces beneath roots are too regular to be natural. The hum seems
  to respond to their presence, hinting at the ark's call without explaining it.
- **Gameplay:** Follow several nearby clues and find a repeated symbol beside
  an inaccessible entrance. Return with a rubbing or sketch, plus an unfamiliar
  specimen found outside the blocked area.
- **Progression:** The library becomes a purposeful destination. The hum also
  has visible or described cues so following it never depends on hearing alone.

### 7. Four places in the old records

- **Story:** Library fragments connect the symbol to four locations. The
  records describe them through inherited names and incomplete diagrams, not a
  convenient explanation of a spaceship. A villager helps interpret the clues.
- **Gameplay:** Identify four long-term destinations and one immediate lead.
  Research and accessible field observations suggest how to cultivate something
  useful for the first entrance.
- **Progression:** The player has a larger mystery and a practical next task.
  The records do not reveal every remedy or replace further exploration.

### 8. First dungeon: the drowned engine

- **Story:** A flooded chamber contains enormous machinery overtaken by the
  darkness. Restoring it proves that something under the island can still work;
  it does not yet explain what the whole structure is.
- **Gameplay:** Develop a wet growing bed and cultivate filtering reeds. Their
  prepared remedy treats the dark seep blocking the entrance. Inside, explore
  waterlogged routes, fight to the source, and use the treatment to cleanse the
  engine before restarting it.
- **Progression:** Engine one runs again. A water conduit also resumes service,
  opening an irrigation option at home. This is an actual ark engine room with
  associated services, not merely a waterworks substituted for an engine.

### 9. The island answers

- **Story:** Back in the village, water flows through an old channel and a
  familiar landmark hums. Different villagers interpret this as good fortune,
  an old story coming true, or a reason for caution.
- **Gameplay:** Put the irrigation discovery to use, improve the farm, and
  spend time with the people affected by the restoration. Revisit library
  fragments with evidence that changes their meaning.
- **Progression:** The next expedition grows out of a visible improvement to
  everyday life. This pause establishes that restoring engines changes home,
  not just a counter on a dungeon checklist.

## Part IV: Discovering the ark

### 10. Second dungeon: the choked engine

- **Story:** Beneath dense jungle growth, another engine chamber is filled with
  darkness-bearing haze. Its structures repeat those of the first chamber,
  suggesting parts of one connected construction.
- **Gameplay:** Use improved irrigation and trellises to cultivate a climbing
  plant with filtering properties. Its preparation clears the entrance's local
  source of haze; inside, open routes and vents at the player's pace, overcome
  the dark creatures, and treat the engine's source.
- **Progression:** Engine two restarts. Restored circulation exposes a route to
  higher terraces where the next useful plant survives. Old carvings connect
  cultivated plants, machinery, and unfamiliar patterns of stars.

### 11. Third dungeon: the cold engine

- **Story:** Beyond the terraces lies an unnaturally cold chamber where the
  darkness has smothered heat and growth. Surviving records suggest the people
  who tended these places were travelling, not simply living underground.
- **Gameplay:** Establish a sheltered growing bed and prepare suitable soil for
  a warmth-bearing plant found on the accessible terraces. Its remedy opens the
  frozen approach and later treats the engine's dark source. Explore and restore
  sections deliberately rather than racing an unavoidable exposure timer.
- **Progression:** Engine three restarts, revealing a working diagram and
  records sufficient to explain the island's nature. Bring that discovery back
  to the village rather than immediately rushing into another dungeon.

### 12. A home that was made to travel

- **Story:** The assembled evidence reveals that the island is an ark, a
  wandering garden stranded on Earth by the darkness. The village's traditions
  take on new meaning. Villagers respond with wonder, uncertainty, and practical
  concerns about what restoring a vessel might mean for their home.
- **Gameplay:** Interpret the discoveries together, revisit changed places, and
  prepare the next stage of the farm. This is a community and discovery
  interlude, not another combat gate.
- **Progression:** The remaining engine is now a step toward freeing a known
  vessel, not merely investigating a strange ruin. The player understands the
  stakes before the final confrontation.

### 13. Fourth dungeon: the lightless engine

- **Story:** The last engine is buried where the darkness has suppressed nearly
  all signs of life. Its condition shows how deeply the ark has been affected,
  without undoing any region the player has already restored.
- **Gameplay:** Develop a small living system rather than just a larger crop:
  a luminous plant supported by a companion groundcover and prepared soil.
  Both species are discoverable outside the sealed dungeon. Grow the remedy,
  cleanse the entrance, then navigate and fight through to the engine's source.
- **Progression:** Engine four restarts. All four engines now operate and their
  combined power opens the route to the control room. The remaining darkness
  there still prevents full control or departure.

## Part V: Answering the call

### 14. The control-room threshold

- **Story:** The newly opened route reaches the outer control room, where the
  protagonist discovers that the ark drew them here, sensing the heart and mind
  that could help it. The means of conveying this remains open: it need not be
  a speaking computer or a complete explanation of the ark's consciousness.
- **Gameplay:** Investigate the remaining obstruction and learn what the final
  treatment requires. The dark heart remains beyond it. This reconnaissance
  ends with a safe return to the village, not a surprise final battle.
- **Progression:** The final objective is understood. Being chosen explains the
  arrival but grants neither an automatic victory nor a replacement for farming.
  It does not, by itself, resolve the cause of the memory loss.

### 15. The final harvest

- **Story:** The community helps prepare for the final expedition using skills
  established throughout the story. The person who first needed shelter now
  belongs to a community undertaking something together.
- **Gameplay:** Develop the final remedy using the growing methods and plants
  already learned. Make this a meaningful cultivation milestone, not an enormous
  quota or a new unrelated crafting system introduced at the last moment.
  Stock provisions and choose when to depart.
- **Progression:** The player is prepared to reach and cure the ark's heart.
  There is no darkness-reclaims-the-village event or imposed deadline to force
  the finale.

### 16. The heart of the darkness

- **Story:** In the inner control room, face the darkness that stranded the ark.
  The precise form and origin of that threat remain open; this outline does
  not assume a humanoid villain, a talking entity, or a sympathetic reversal.
- **Gameplay:** A final encounter brings together exploration and combat skills.
  Overcoming the creatures or manifestation provides access to the source; the
  farm-developed remedy vanquishes it. Treatment is a deliberate action, not a
  timing-critical story input.
- **Progression:** The darkness is defeated, all four engines are clean and
  running, and the ark comes under full control. The cure, not merely the last
  weapon strike, completes the restoration.

## Part VI: Carrying home forward

### 17. Preparing to leave

- **Story:** The village prepares for departure. Earlier hopes and concerns
  receive a response through character moments, not a last-minute assumption
  that nobody minds leaving. The home, farm, and community will travel together.
- **Gameplay:** Allow time to visit people, tend the farm, and finish personal
  preparations. Give a clear, player-initiated launch interaction so defeating
  the darkness does not unexpectedly remove control and end the game.
- **Progression:** Launch reveals familiar parts of the island as components of
  the ark. The agreed ending is departure, not a newly introduced stay-or-leave
  branch. Post-ending free play and save behaviour remain separate decisions.

### 18. The first green on another world

- **Story:** A short epilogue shows life from the restored wandering garden
  taking root on another world. Echo the first small planting in the village,
  now carrying a much larger meaning.
- **Gameplay:** A brief planting interaction could provide the final player
  action; whether this is playable or cinematic remains open. Do not introduce
  a second planet's progression systems.
- **Resolution:** The castaway who first struggled to survive has helped a
  community and its living home bring life elsewhere. The darkness conflict and
  the ark's interrupted purpose receive an ending, not just a sequel hook.

## Rules for developing this outline

- **Access before ingredients:** Every required seed, cultivation clue, and
  farm improvement must be obtainable before the gate it unlocks. The four
  engines form a proposed sequence, but no dependency may require entry to the
  very room it is meant to open.
- **Entrance treatment is not dungeon completion:** Clearing a local entrance
  creates a permanent foothold. Reaching and curing the separate source at the
  engine is still necessary to restart it.
- **Recoverable preparation:** Losing a crop, remedy, or expedition must not
  strand progression. Essential materials need a renewable or recoverable
  source; exact recovery and defeat rules remain to be designed.
- **Different development, not four colours of the same key:** Wet cultivation,
  irrigation and trellises, sheltered growing, and a companion ecosystem are
  proposed ways to make the four farm milestones distinct. Their produce should
  retain useful roles beyond opening one door.
- **Engines, not four unrelated facilities:** All four dungeons contain real
  engines needed for the ark to travel. Water, circulation, heat, and light
  provide environmental identity and restoration effects; detailed engineering
  is not established here.
- **Home remains relevant:** Return visits should change relationships,
  cultivation possibilities, or understanding. Not every engine needs a
  separate mandatory village quest before the next expedition.
- **No unearned authority:** Villagers have skills and contribute knowledge.
  The protagonist being chosen does not mean everyone else is incapable.
- **Perceptible clues and deliberate actions:** Pair sound-based clues with
  visual or textual evidence. Required story progress must not depend on
  hearing, colour alone, or timing-critical inputs.

## Decisions still needed

- Whether these stages have the right pacing, especially the first remedy,
  the village interludes, and the ark reveal after the third engine.
- Whether the engine sequence is fixed as proposed or allows some player choice.
- Which proposed dungeon environments and farming methods to keep.
- The villagers, their relationships, and the specific traditions and records.
- The nature of the darkness, the ark's unseen power, and the memory-loss answer.
- The length and style of dungeons, combat, and individual restoration encounters.
- How each engine's activation changes the overworld without creating busywork.
- How to handle saving, unfinished activities, and play after the ending.
