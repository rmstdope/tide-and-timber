Building a camp — agreed

The outline is placed just in front of him with E or a click; then the screen fades to black for about 5 seconds and the thing stands, 30 minutes later. One camp only; the fire goes out at 07:00, leaving ash. Each picture is the game's 320×180 screen; art, colours and positions are indicative.
