Moving the game onto the Pixel Crawler pack — what was chosen

Clothes, hair and recolouring in these drawings are rough automatic stand-ins for hand-painted art.

  • Colours: Palette A — the pack's colours become the game's; our palm, driftwood, spring, shellfish, coconuts, icons and HUD are repainted in them
  • The man: Hero A — the pack's full figure (Body_A), dressed as a castaway
  • Order: Order A — island, then man, then icons and HUD
  • His look: Look 1 — dark hair and stubble, pale torn shirt, blue rolled trousers, bare feet
  • Waking: Wake 1 — his collapse played backwards
  • HUD: HUD 1 — same bar and shape, colours only
  • Carrying: Option A — not yet; the carrying pose is left for a later piece of work where he really holds something
