Question 2 — which of the pack's figures plays the stranded man?

Each figure below is moving the way it would in the game, shown 4× larger on the pack's sand, with today's man on the left for comparison. Under each is what the game needs him to do and whether the pack already has it.

Hero A — the full figure, dressed as a castaway (recommended)

The pack's full figure, with a torn shirt and rolled trousers painted over every frame. The clothes in this drawing are a rough automatic stand-in; the real ones would be hand-painted once and repeated on each pose.
Why recommended: it is the only figure with every move the story needs already drawn, and a stranded sailor should look like one, not a bare mannequin.

Hero B — the full figure as it comes

Same moves as A, nothing painted on: a bald, bare-skinned figure. No extra art work, but he reads as a placeholder, not a person.

Hero C — the pack's newer figure

Only standing and walking exist, and they're still rough coloured blocks (head, body, legs) — the pack's artist hasn't finished it. Everything else he does would have to be drawn from scratch.
