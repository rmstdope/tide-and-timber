Frames on boards, plain plates for rows

Every board that holds things (the clock, the item bar, the pause board, settings, question boxes, the build list, the morning card) gets the knobbed frame. Everything inside a board, and every small thing floating over the world (menu rows, title rows, the item name, the use prompt), is a plain two-colour plate. Rows stay today's height; the chosen row turns light wood with a pale edge, as today. Words on the build list and the morning card are stand-ins; their real words do not change.
