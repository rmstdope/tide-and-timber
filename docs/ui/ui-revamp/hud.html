UI revamp: four ways to draw the same screen

Every picture holds the same things in the same places: the item bar with a selected coconut and its name, the day clock, a needs meter, and an open pack. The needs meter and the pack do not exist in the game today and the vision leaves needs undecided; they are here only so each style can be judged on the pieces it would have to carry. Item icons: today's 10x10 in A, Farming 101's 16x16 coconut and shell in B to D, where the slots have room for them.

A. Today: square wooden planks, cream slots, drawn in code

B. Retro Inventory as shipped: charcoal and gold

Rounded 22 px slots in a knobbed frame, the heart-capped bar. Reads as dungeon gear; the darkest thing on a warm beach.

C. Retro Inventory's shapes in today's wood and cream

The same pieces with their seven colours swapped for the HUD's own: the rounded slots, the knobbed corners and the hearts stay, the dungeon goes. Needs five hearts from the pack's five-state heart.

D. Farming 101's tan tray and panel

The pack's 18 px slot tray (shipped with six slots, shown joined to eight), its soft-cornered tan panel and its 9x7 hearts. The lightest touch, closest in size to today.
