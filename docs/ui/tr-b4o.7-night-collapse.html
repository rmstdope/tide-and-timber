Collapsing at night — agreed

From 21:00 to 05:00 he must stay in his camp's firelight. Stepping out brings frost and a line; about 5 seconds outside and he collapses. He falls, the screen goes black with his line, and he wakes at 06:00 beside his lean-to (or where he first woke), having lost half of each kind he carried, shown on a card that fades by itself. Each picture is the game's 320×180 screen; art, colours and positions are indicative.
